SyntaxHue MVP mockups

High-fidelity screens for the MVP in BACKLOG.md, drawn to the tokens and components in DESIGN.md. Numbered dots match the notes under each screen. Desktop frames are 1280×800 scaled to fit.

S1 · First visit, exploring

A fresh shuffle in the visitor's system mode. Shuffle is the primary action and the first coach mark explains Space and locks.

  • 1Shuffle is primary until the user locks or edits a color.
  • 2Coach mark 1 of 3 never blocks interaction; Skip ends the tour.
  • 3Language switcher above the preview (HP-505); the preview scales to fit (HP-509).
  • 4Rail footer summarizes readability for the whole theme.

S2 · Editing a role, with vibe

The user clicked a keyword in the preview: the rail opens Keyword, its uses are outlined, and Export has become the primary action. The Vibe popover is open on top.

  • 1Export turns primary after the first lock or edit; Shuffle steps down.
  • 2Dashed orange outline shows every use of the open role.
  • 3Inline contrast fix nudges lightness only and is one undo step.
  • 4Vibe sliders are centered at 0 with named ends and per-slider reset.
  • 5Locked roles show a filled lock; the footer links to the failing role.
  • 6Saved state next to the name: "Edited, not saved".

S4 · Export, right after copying

The instructions become a three-step visual guide once the snippet is on the clipboard.

  • 1Same verb through the flow: "Copy snippet" becomes "Copied snippet".
  • 2Scoped by default so other themes stay untouched.
  • 3Troubleshooting line covers the most likely failure: an existing customizations block.

S5 · Library, light chrome

The library drawer over a light theme. The chrome follows the theme's mode. Deleting shows an undo toast instead of a confirmation.

  • 1Update vs save as new: the button names the theme it will overwrite.
  • 2Each theme shows a strip of six key colors, its mode and when it was last edited.
  • 3Undo toast after delete, 5 s, pauses on hover.

S8 · Mobile

Preview on top, a scrolling swatch strip and a thumb bar at the bottom. Tapping a swatch or the preview opens a bottom sheet that keeps the preview visible.

  • 1Long-press a swatch to lock it; locked swatches get a dot.
  • 2Bottom sheet at 55 % height with larger slider thumbs (44 px targets).
